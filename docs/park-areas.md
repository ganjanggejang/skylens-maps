# Park area inference

`Category` tokens identify park facilities in `Building_Boundary.json`; `Parking` alone is not a park. The data has no park boundary polygons, so the map initially displays individual park footprints in pale green.

After the map is ready, `src/data/parks.worker.ts` derives enclosed blocks from ground-level road centerlines. Curves are simplified within 20 cm, intersections and overlapping segments are noded, and directed edges are traversed to find bounded faces. Paths, tunnels and elevated roads do not divide a park. Road corridors use the exported road width, with an 8 m fallback when width is unavailable. Integer centimeter polygon operations remove these corridors, cul-de-sacs and disconnected inner road loops from the block interior. Water renders above the park fill.

A park-only block qualifies when its park footprints span at least 55% of the usable interior in both coordinate directions and cover at least 10% of its area. Facility count is irrelevant: two facilities at opposite ends or one substantial park can establish the same spatial evidence. Non-park overlap must stay within a 4 square meter tolerance. For blocks with more non-park overlap, the original conservative rule remains: at least three broadly distributed park footprints, at least 15% coverage, and non-park footprints occupying at most 2% of the park footprint area. Independently, park coverage of at least 45% can qualify without broad distribution, subject to the same non-park limit. Overlapping park footprints are unioned before calculating coverage. Blocks smaller than 100 square meters or larger than 2 square kilometers are not expanded. These thresholds are an inference policy, not exported game boundaries.

Qualifying blocks replace contained individual footprints; other parks retain their own polygons with road space removed. Park fills are beneath buildings and roads. Park facility footprints no longer receive the generic beige fill or outline, while their original feature IDs, selection highlights and existing icons remain available.

The worker runs once per loaded dataset, is terminated after returning its result, and is cancelled when the map is disposed. If inference fails, the initial individual footprints remain visible. The geometry library is bundled only in the worker. `src/data/park-areas.test.ts` covers closed/open boundaries, mixed-use blocks, isolated and scattered parks, intersections, cul-de-sacs, nested loops and holes using synthetic data.
